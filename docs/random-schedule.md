# Random weekly publication policy

Use America/Chicago calendar weeks (Monday–Sunday) and calendar months. Choose 2 or 3 publication opportunities per week, randomly; never publish more than 3 in a week. Maintain at least 48 elapsed hours between actual publications, including across week and month boundaries. Fifteen posts per calendar month is a ceiling, not an exact target: this cadence typically produces fewer. Source freshness, review, permissions and plan quota always take precedence over filling slots.

The host wakes daily at 09:00 Chicago to inspect a persisted weekly plan. This is an internal planning trigger, not a promise of daily posting. At the first run of a new week, choose the week's target count randomly from [2,3] with equal probability. Enumerate combinations of remaining local dates that meet the 48-hour gap using proposed 09:00 slots and the last confirmed publication timestamp. Include confirmed posts and any scheduled/pending posts in weekly/monthly counts. Choose randomly from feasible combinations, persist the plan under runtime/schedule/YYYY-Www.json before doing external work, and never redraw an existing plan merely because the task reran. Use an OS random generator; save selected slots and target, not invented evidence of randomization.

At migration midweek, account for posts already published or scheduled and choose only remaining dates. Prefer the selected count when feasible, otherwise reduce to a feasible count and record why. A partial week, failure or lack of fresh news can legitimately produce fewer than 2 posts. Do not backfill by crowding posts together. Do not assign topics until the selected day's live research run.

On nonselected days, stay quiet except meaningful failures requiring attention. On a selected day, reconcile unresolved submissions first, check live account quota and ledger, then research and generate the post. Recheck the 48-hour interval against actual publication timestamps immediately before submission; generation delays can invalidate a later slot. If a slot is blocked, mark it skipped and optionally move it to an unused later day in the same week only if all spacing/count/freshness constraints still pass. Never compress gaps, add a fourth post, or carry a missed target into next week. An unknown publish outcome blocks another write until reconciled.

Manual posts on this account also count when known. Refresh available published/scheduled records before deciding; missing publication timestamps or inaccessible state must not be interpreted as zero recent posts. Temporary review drafts stay excluded. Fifteen is an account publication cap, not a license to exceed the Publora quota. At the cap skip remaining slots until the next local calendar month, still respecting the 48-hour interval. Month-boundary weeks share the same weekly maximum.

For hosts with daily scheduling, run this planning gate daily and keep publication inside selected occurrences. The current implementation is host-agent orchestration with persisted runtime state, not a standalone scheduling daemon. Actual times follow research and review; dates are randomized, while the internal wake time stays at 09:00. The plan must survive retries and be protected by the host's single-writer policy. Report confirmed publication only with a live post identifier/URL.
