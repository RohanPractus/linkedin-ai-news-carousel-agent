# Humanizer, hashtags and analytics

The humanizer aims for readable prose, not AI-detector evasion. Remove stock openers, filler, staged sincerity and excessive rhetorical fragments. Preserve the actual claim, date/version, vendor attribution, hypothetical labels, limitations and numbers. A sourced post does not need a fabricated personal story.

Regression example: a hypothetical agent completes 18 of 20 tasks and two failures create duplicate records. The rewrite must retain all those qualifications, remove an unsupported universal staff-replacement claim and introduce no measured benchmark or personal deployment. The private workflow passed 12 explicit checks on this case; that is a behavioral test, not a claim of human authorship or improved reach.

Use natural keywords and 2–3 relevant topical hashtags as an editorial starting point. Select tags from the current event, not the monthly category. Drug discovery should not inherit robotics hashtags. Never claim a hashtag is popular or creates reach without evidence. LinkedIn's official feed guidance emphasizes relevant professional content and authentic conversations; it does not certify this count as optimal.

Record impressions, reached members, reactions, comments and reshares when available; include observation time and age. Engagement rate is (reactions+comments+reshares)/impressions; missing or zero denominator is unavailable. Public comments cannot reveal impressions. Current Publora LinkedIn analytics may require REST and account/plan access; generic MCP post_stats is not a substitute.

Compare similar topics/formats/windows with sample size, median outcomes and uncertainty. Observational comparisons cannot establish causal hashtag lift. Do not artificially boost engagement with automated comments, likes or DMs.
